Anastasios Vlachos

PhD Student | Systems, Control and Optimization Group, University of Groningen | a.vlachos(at)rug(dot)nl | Google Scholar

I am a PhD student at the System, Control and Optimization Group at the University of Groningen, supervised by Prof. Bart Besselink. I am currently working on the modular design of control systems using Contract Theory.

Previously, I obtained the MSc in Systems and Control from ETH Zürich. During my studies, I worked at the Automatic Control Laboratory (IfA) on learning-based (online) control for tracking. A public-friendly overview of my work can be found here.

I completed my undergraduate studies in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the National Technical University of Athens.
